Child safety standards 🐾

VoterPup (voterpup.com and the VoterPup app on Google Play). Published October 6, 2026. Contact: hi@voterpup.com

Zero tolerance. VoterPup prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind: posting, sharing, linking to, requesting or soliciting it, and any grooming or sexualisation of minors. Accounts involved are removed and reported.

Who VoterPup is for

VoterPup is a civic app for adults (18 and over). It is not designed for or directed at children.

How we prevent it

  • Public photos are checked before anyone sees them. Every photo on a shared issue is reviewed automatically before it appears publicly. Photos with nudity or sexual content, or with a child as the clear subject, are blocked.
  • Videos are never shown publicly.
  • Messages are checked automatically in groups and on public walls before they are shown, and sexual content is blocked.
  • Groups are small and invite-only. A group holds up to 12 pups, invite links expire after one hour, and the group's owner approves each person. There is no way to message strangers.
  • No public profiles and no searching for other users.

How to report

  • In the app: tap Report on a message or post. Content reported by two people is hidden at once, and we review every report.
  • By email: write to hi@voterpup.com with the subject “Child safety”. Include a link or a description, but please do not attach the material itself.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in Canada and the US) first.

What we do

  • Remove the content and the account (pup) responsible, and keep evidence only as the law requires.
  • Report apparent CSAM to the authorities: in Canada to Cybertip.ca (Canadian Centre for Child Protection), in the US to the NCMEC CyberTipline, and to police where required.
  • Cooperate with law enforcement requests made through proper legal process.

Contact

Our designated contact for child safety is hi@voterpup.com. See also our privacy policy.
