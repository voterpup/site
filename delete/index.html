Delete your VoterPup data 🐾

For the VoterPup app on Google Play and voterpup.com. Questions: hi@voterpup.com

On VoterPup your account is your pup. There is no username or password; the pup opens through its private link.

Delete everything yourself (immediate)

  1. Open VoterPup and go to your pup (the My pup tab).
  2. Tap ⚙️ (top right).
  3. Tap “Delete [your pup’s name] and all its data” and confirm.

This happens at once. No request or waiting period.

Or ask us

Email hi@voterpup.com with your pup’s link (or the email address you gave your pup). We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm. You can also ask us to delete only some data, for example one issue, one photo, or your email address, and keep the rest.

What is deleted

  • Your pup and its name
  • Every issue you added, private or shared, and their photos and videos
  • Your “same here”, “fixed” and emoji reactions
  • Your groups’ memberships and your messages; groups you own are closed
  • Your Guess the wish games, answers and plays
  • Your reminders, notification sign-ups and any email address you gave for reports or game emails
  • Usage counts recorded against your pup

What is kept

  • Totals with nothing linking them to you, such as how many people chose an issue in a city ranking. They contain no name, email or text of yours.
  • Google backup sign-in: if you backed up your pup with Google, the sign-in record holding your email address is removed when you email us to ask.
  • Routine backups: deleted data can remain in our hosting provider’s encrypted backups for up to 30 days, until they are overwritten.

City 3-1-1 reports shown in the app are the City of Vancouver’s public records, not your data, and are not affected.
