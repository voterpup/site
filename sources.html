Sources of government information

VoterPup (voterpup.com and the VoterPup app on Google Play). Updated October 8, 2026. Contact: hi@voterpup.com

VoterPup is not a government app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any government, election authority or city. It does not provide government services. For anything official (registering, where and how to vote, ballots, results, service requests), always use the official sources below.

Election dates, voting information and ballot questions

VoterPup shows upcoming election dates and ballot questions so people remember to vote. They come from these official sources:

  • City of Vancouver: 2026 municipal election, Voters' Guide: vancouver.ca/your-government/2026-voters-guide.aspx
  • City of Vancouver: 2026 capital plan borrowing and plebiscite questions: vancouver.ca/your-government/2026-capital-plan-borrowing-questions-and-plebiscite-questions.aspx
  • City of Vancouver: speaking at council meetings: vancouver.ca/your-government/speak-at-city-council-meetings.aspx
  • City of Vancouver public engagement (Shape Your City): shapeyourcity.ca
  • Elections BC (provincial elections in British Columbia): elections.bc.ca
  • Elections Canada (federal elections in Canada): elections.ca
  • United States (registering and voting in every state, from the U.S. government): vote.gov

City 3-1-1 service requests

Some cities publish the service requests residents send through 3-1-1 as open data. VoterPup shows these records as published, marked “City 3-1-1”, with only the block or neighbourhood, never a house number. Sources:

  • Vancouver: City of Vancouver Open Data, 3-1-1 service requests (Open Government Licence – Vancouver): opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/3-1-1-service-requests
  • Seattle: City of Seattle Open Data, Customer Service Requests: data.seattle.gov/d/5ngg-rpne
  • San Francisco: DataSF, 311 Cases: data.sfgov.org/d/vw6y-z8j6
  • New York City: NYC Open Data, 311 Service Requests: data.cityofnewyork.us/d/erm2-nwe9
  • Chicago: City of Chicago Data Portal, 311 Service Requests: data.cityofchicago.org/d/v6vf-nfxy
  • Austin: City of Austin Open Data, 311 Service Requests: data.austintexas.gov/d/xwdj-i9he
  • Calgary: City of Calgary Open Data, 311 Service Requests (Open Government Licence – City of Calgary): data.calgary.ca/d/iahh-g8bj
  • Edmonton: City of Edmonton Open Data, 311 Requests: data.edmonton.ca/d/q7ua-agfg

Everything else

Issues, lists and messages that people write in VoterPup are their own words, not government information. To report a problem to your city, use your city's official 3-1-1 service. If you see something on VoterPup that does not match its official source, email hi@voterpup.com and we will correct it.
