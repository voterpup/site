Why VoterPup exists

By election day, most of us can't remember the things that bugged us all year. Politicians count on that — it's why campaigns run on whatever is loudest in the final week, not on what your street actually needs.

VoterPup is the memory: five seconds to log what's broken, a pup that never forgets it, and a nudge the moment you can actually act on it — a vote, a council decision, a public hearing.

And when enough remembered issues add up, something bigger happens: the loudest thing stops winning by default, and what people actually care about becomes impossible to ignore.
