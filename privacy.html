VoterPup privacy 🐾

Plain language. Last updated September 29, 2026 (v7). Questions: hi@voterpup.com

The short version: no account is needed. We never sell your data, never show ads, and never tell anyone how to vote. What you give your pup is private unless you choose to share it.

What we store

  • Your pup and what you tell it: the pup's name, your entries (text), photos or videos you attach, the date, a topic label, and the city you logged it from.
  • City, not coordinates: if you allow location, your browser's coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's geocoding service (Nominatim) to look up your city name. If you type a city instead, that search text goes to the same service. We store only the city, region and country — never your coordinates.
  • Pinning an issue to a spot (optional, per issue): if you tap 📍 while writing an issue, your coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap once to get a street name, and the street name plus coordinates (rounded to about 10 metres) are stored with that issue only. If you share that issue, the spot is shown with it. Don’t pin issues at your own home unless you want that known.
  • Reactions: “Same here”, “Still broken” and “Looks fixed” are stored as counts against the issue, with your pup’s link so you can change your mind. They are shown as numbers only.
  • Reports by email (optional): if you give an email address for your pup’s reports, we send one short email a day at the time you chose, and one on the morning you vote. Every email has an unsubscribe link. The address is stored with your pup, used for nothing else, and deleted when you unsubscribe or your pup is deleted. Emails are sent through Resend.
  • Google sign-in loads only when you tap backup or restore. Until then, no Google code runs on our pages.
  • Email, only if you choose Google backup: if you back up a pup with Google, our authentication provider stores your email address so you can restore your pup on another device. We use it for nothing else — no newsletters, no marketing.

Private vs. shared

  • Private (the default): entries are visible only through your pup's private link. They never appear on the public board. Topic labels for private entries are assigned inside our own database, so the text is not sent to any outside service.
  • Shared ("Be counted"): the entry's text, photos, topic and city appear on the public board (The Pack), signed with your pup's name — never your real name or email. Shared text is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to assign a topic label. You can make a shared entry private again at any time.
  • Photos are checked before anyone else sees them: before a photo appears publicly (on The Pack, or on a read-only code you give a candidate), it is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to check for nudity, violence, hate symbols, readable personal documents and photos centred on children. Until that check passes, the public sees “photo under review”. Videos are never shown publicly. Photos on entries only you can see are not sent for this check.

Your private link is a key

  • Your pup has no password — its link is the key. Anyone who has the link (or scans your sticker's QR code) can read that pup's entries. Keep it like a key.
  • Photos and videos are stored privately. They're shown only through short-lived links (they expire within an hour): to people viewing your pup through its link, or on the public board if you shared that entry.

Where your data lives

  • Our database and file storage are hosted by Supabase in the Canada (Central) region.
  • Our website is served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare.
  • We count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and never records your private pup link.
  • Usage counts: the app records which features are used (a pup was made, an issue added, a reminder turned on, the app opened) against a random device number it makes itself. No name, email, location or text goes with these counts. They tell us whether VoterPup is working.
  • Our earlier survey form (voterpup.com home page) saves answers and any email you typed there to a Google Sheet we control.

Voting information

Election dates, ballot questions and consultation deadlines come from official public sources (for example the City of Vancouver, Elections BC and the City's Shape Your City site), which we link to. When someone uses VoterPup in a city we don’t cover yet, we record that city’s name (never who asked) and an automated search looks for its upcoming votes on official government and election-authority websites. A date is shown only after we confirm it word-for-word on that official page. Always confirm details with the official source before you vote.

Your choices

  • Make any shared entry private again, from your pup's page.
  • Ask us to delete your pup, entries, photos or backup email: email hi@voterpup.com with your pup's link.
  • Ask what we hold about you, or correct it, the same way.
