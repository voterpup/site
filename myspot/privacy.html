Memspots privacy 📍

Plain language. Last updated October 27, 2026. Questions: hi@voterpup.com

The short version: places remember, people stay private. We read your location only at the moment you tap, compare it once, and throw it away. We never sell or share personal information and never show ads. A shop may pay to feature its own wall on the map; when it does, it is marked Promoted, and it never changes what is on any wall or who can leave a memory.

Location

  • Read once, when you tap. Memspots asks for your location only when you leave a memory or open one. Never in the background, never while you walk around.
  • Compared, then discarded. Your position is compared with the memory’s spot once to decide whether it opens. We do not store where you stood and we keep no history of where you have been.
  • A memory keeps its own spot. When you leave a memory, it is stored with the location of that memory, because that is where it opens.

Your account

  • Reading needs no account. Opening a public memory or a shop’s wall works without signing in.
  • Leaving a memory needs a sign-in with Google or a code sent to your email. We keep your name and profile photo so they can appear with your memory, and nothing else from your account. Your email is never shown to anyone.
  • Hearts are stored as a count against your account, or against a random number your phone makes if you are not signed in. Never your name.

What you leave

  • Your memory is yours. You can change who sees it, hide it, or delete it for good at any time.
  • Checked before it opens. Every memory, photo and note is checked automatically for hate, threats, sexual content, harassment, personal details such as addresses and phone numbers, scams and graphic images before anyone can open it. The text and photo are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) for that check and are not kept there.
  • Photos are stored privately and shown only through short-lived links, to people standing at the spot or, for a shop’s wall, to people inside the shop.
  • People in your photos. When you post a photo on a shop’s wall, you confirm that everyone in it is okay with that, and that a parent or guardian said yes for any child. We keep the time you confirmed. Anyone in a photo who wants it down can ask you, the shop, or us.
  • Deleted means deleted. When you delete a memory, the photo file and the post are erased from our storage, not hidden. If it was on a shop’s wall, the shop is told that the post is gone.

Who owns what

  • You own what you leave. You give Memspots a licence to store it, check it, and show it where you chose: at its spot, on a shop’s wall, and on the map if you made it public. That licence passes to whoever runs Memspots after us, so your memories keep working.
  • We never sell your memories, license them to anyone else, or put them in advertising.
  • A shop owns its wall: its name, note, picture, settings and the copy it downloads. A memory left on a shop’s wall may be shown there and kept in the shop’s copy, and that is all the shop may do with it.
  • Memspots owns the app and the counts and patterns drawn from all memories with nobody identifiable, such as how many people visited a park this month.

Shops and their walls

  • A shop that owns a wall sees counts: how many people opened it, how many came back, and the posts on its wall with their hearts. It can pin a post, hide it (undoable) or remove it for good, photo included.
  • A shop can choose to approve posts before they show. Until then, a waiting post is seen only by the shop and by the person who left it.
  • A shop never sees who opened its wall, and never sees anyone’s email or location.
  • A shop can download its whole wall, text and photos, as a PDF or a PowerPoint at any time. If a shop stops using Memspots, or Memspots closes, the shop gets a copy first, then its wall and everything on it is permanently deleted. A shop can ask for that deletion at any time.
  • If a shop asks for a weekly email, we send one every Monday to the address the owner gave us, and a short note when a post is waiting for approval or a guest removed one. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Anything we publish about places is aggregate and anonymous, never about an individual.

Where your data lives

  • Our database and file storage are hosted by Supabase in the Canada (Central) region.
  • Our website is served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Maps come from OpenStreetMap.
  • Emails (sign-in codes, the ones you ask for, and shop reports) are sent through Resend.
  • We count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies. Usage counts (a memory left, a wall opened) are recorded against a random device number, with no name, email, location or text attached.

Your choices

  • Delete any memory you left from the Mine page, at any time.
  • Unsubscribe from any email with the link at the bottom of it.
  • Export or delete everything you have left with one request to hi@voterpup.com.
